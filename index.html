THE CHALLENGE

Why Optical Alignment Matters

Free-Space Optical Communication relies on highly accurate pointing between transmitter and receiver. Even small pointing errors can reduce link quality or cause the optical link to be lost.

🎯

Initial Pointing Error

The transmitter and receiver may initially be misaligned, making rapid target acquisition essential.

〰️

Motion & Disturbances

Platform movement, vibration and target motion can continuously change the pointing direction.

🔭

Precise Tracking

The system must continuously estimate pointing error and correct the optical axis toward the beacon.

SYSTEM WORKFLOW

How HELIOS Works

HELIOS follows a closed-loop tracking approach in which the beacon position is detected, the pointing error is calculated and the pan/tilt system continuously corrects it.

PROTOTYPE CAPABILITIES

Built for Real-Time Experimentation

The browser-based prototype allows different tracking conditions to be tested without requiring physical hardware during the initial development stage.

🎯 Dynamic Target Tracking Test circular, sinusoidal and random beacon trajectories to evaluate tracking response.
⚡ Disturbance Simulation Introduce artificial disturbances to observe how the controller responds to sudden pointing errors.
📡 Beacon Loss Detection Simulate beacon disappearance and observe the system transition into a target-lost state.
🎛️ Controller Tuning Adjust proportional gain, target speed, noise and lock radius to study system behaviour.
